A long time ago, in a land, far far away ... The Cosmic Spark

Rajesh lived in a small town, where life was a constant struggle. His family had very little money, and every day brought a new challenge. To help support his family, Rajesh spent his days cleaning the windshields of cars stopped at traffic signals.

Despite his difficult circumstances, Rajesh was curious about the world around him. He often wondered how things worked and dreamed of inventing something that could make people's lives easier.

One night, while returning home, Rajesh witnessed a spectacular meteor shower. The sky was filled with streaks of light, and he found himself thinking about the mysteries of the universe.

Inspired by that moment, Rajesh began experimenting with different materials and ideas. He wanted to create something useful, affordable, and accessible to everyone.

After many attempts, he developed a low-cost fan that could provide relief from the heat without requiring expensive equipment. His invention was simple, but it had the potential to make a real difference in people's lives.

And so, from a small idea born under a sky full of stars, Rajesh began his journey towards something extraordinary.
